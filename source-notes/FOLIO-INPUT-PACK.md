# Year 8 Agriculture - twelve-card folio input pack

Status: exact content and field specification for TASK 11 integration. No formal weighting or submission status is implied.

## Shared rules

- Use exactly twelve cards in the order below.
- Cards 1-11 mirror the authoritative 2026 sequence. Card 12 is a gated course-synthesis shell. The older workbook and poultry booklet are supplementary/reference only and do not create a current requirement.
- Each card includes a saved substantive response, an evidence caption/source, completion criteria and a reflection/next step.
- Images are optional unless the teacher makes them required. Any image must be authorised under school privacy/device rules.
- Photos do not replace teacher observation, animal-care supervision or local practical checks.
- Browser-local evidence is preparation only, not automatic submission.
- Never prefill project dimensions, scale, quantities, locations, practical procedures, animal-care controls, safety rules or teacher approvals.

## Card specifications

### 1. Agriculture in daily life and industry sectors

**Purpose:** define agriculture and connect it to everyday products and producer sectors.

**Student inputs:** definition in own words; five daily agricultural products; sector/purpose/example table; chosen sector; why it matters; evidence source/caption; one question still held.

**Completion:** five products, at least four completed sector rows and a justified chosen-sector response.

**Source:** 2026 Introduction to Agriculture lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 2. Agriculture, sustainability and food security

**Purpose:** explain why agriculture matters locally, economically and for food security.

**Student inputs:** agriculture-importance explanation; supported statistic/source; local/export impact; food-security definition; four components; two threats; one response or resilience idea.

**Completion:** all four components, two supported threats and a cause/effect explanation.

**Source:** 2026 Value of Agriculture & Food Security lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-SDP-01, TE4-PDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 3. Beef breeds and adaptation

**Purpose:** classify beef breeds and explain how breed characteristics suit environments and production goals.

**Student inputs:** Bos indicus/Bos taurus comparison; breed identification record; chosen breed and three verified facts; climate/adaptation explanation; selective-breeding benefit and limitation; image/source caption.

**Completion:** both cattle groups distinguished and the chosen breed linked to an environmental or production reason.

**Source:** 2026 Introduction to Beef Cattle & Breeds lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-SDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 4. Build a beef farm - system decision

**Purpose:** apply taught knowledge to a simple agricultural-system design decision.

**Student inputs:** extensive/intensive choice; proposed climate/location category; breed category; feeding method; system diagram or layout sketch; three design reasons; one sustainability trade-off; unresolved item.

**Completion:** choices are internally consistent and justified with at least three taught facts.

**Source:** 2026 Beef Cattle Farming & Nutrition lesson plan/worksheet and `Build a Beef Farm` activity.

**Boundary:** this is a conceptual system design, not an approved property or infrastructure plan.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PPM-01, TE4-DES-01, TE4-SDP-01.

### 5. Dairy breeds, regions and farm routines

**Purpose:** explain the relationship between breed, region, milk production and value adding.

**Student inputs:** breed/features evidence; labelled region map or region table; two suitability factors; source-linked farm-routine responses; value-adding definition; three products; caption/source.

**Completion:** a breed and region are each linked to a supported production reason.

**Source:** 2026 Dairy Breeds & Milk Production lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-SDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 6. Milk processing and nutrition flow

**Purpose:** communicate how milk is made safer/consistent and connect dairy foods to nutrition.

**Student inputs:** pasteurisation and homogenisation explanations; ordered cow-to-consumer flowchart; nutrient/role table; dairy-alternative consideration; challenge explanation; source/caption.

**Completion:** the processing flow is correctly ordered and at least three nutrients are linked to roles.

**Source:** 2026 Dairy Processing & Nutrition lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 7. Horticulture regions and growing conditions

**Purpose:** explain how climate, water, soil and management shape horticulture regions.

**Student inputs:** horticulture definition/products; intensive-system explanation; region-description match; labelled map or accessible region table; two growing factors with explanations; region/product justification.

**Completion:** two factors are explained and one crop is justified for one region.

**Source:** 2026 What Is Horticulture & Where Do We Grow It? lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-SDP-01, TE4-PDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 8. Paddock to plate, robotics and healthy choice

**Purpose:** trace a horticultural product through a system and evaluate the role of technology and nutrition.

**Student inputs:** paddock-to-plate sequence; two farm challenges; three technology benefits; RIPPA job/benefit record; future-work reflection; plant-part table; nutrition/seasonality/variety explanation.

**Completion:** the supply chain is ordered, technology is linked to a farm challenge and a healthy-choice explanation is present.

**Source:** 2026 From Paddock to Plate - Technology, Nutrition & Choice lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-SDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 9. Australian pork, labels and breeding

**Purpose:** connect production, consumer labels, industry evidence and breeding choices.

**Student inputs:** fresh/value-added products; sourced industry facts; fresh/processed origin distinction; two product-label observations; buy-Australian reasoning; breed examples; crossbreeding/hybrid-vigour explanation.

**Completion:** at least two actual label observations and one evidence-based breeding explanation.

**Source:** 2026 Introduction to Pork and the Australian Pork Industry lesson plan and worksheet.

**Boundary:** time-sensitive figures must retain their lesson source/date or be refreshed before use.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-SDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 10. Pig production systems, feeding and welfare

**Purpose:** compare production systems and justify a choice using welfare, efficiency and feeding evidence.

**Student inputs:** four-system comparison; preferred system; two reasons; different-farmer explanation; feed/growth responses; two stress causes; care/meat-quality explanation; source/caption.

**Completion:** all four systems compared and the preferred system justified with at least two criteria.

**Source:** 2026 Pig Farming Systems and Feeding lesson plan and worksheet.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-SDP-01, TE4-PDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 11. Poultry production systems and trade-offs

**Purpose:** explain broiler/layer production, compare layer systems and make an evidence-based welfare judgement.

**Student inputs:** poultry definition/species; broiler/layer comparison; farm-location factors; cage/barn/free-range table; best-welfare judgement using at least three currently verified facts; welfare/cost/productivity/demand trade-off record; optional practical evidence only if teacher confirmed.

**Completion:** all three layer systems are compared and the judgement cites at least three taught facts.

**Source:** 2026 Australian Poultry Industry lesson plan/worksheet. The older poultry booklet is supplementary/reference only unless the teacher confirms a current lesson dependence.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PDP-01, TE4-SDP-01, TE4-DES-01.

### 12. Agriculture plot plan, practical evidence and final evaluation

**Purpose:** provide a course-synthesis, plan-reading and practical-evidence shell without inventing a missing plan, requirement or local procedure.

**Student inputs supported now:** final course strength, supporting evidence, limitation, improvement and transferable learning drawn from Cards 1-11.

**Additional inputs only after teacher confirmation:** student-drawn school agriculture-plot plan; title/legend/orientation used; named areas/tools; tool name/use record; dated practical evidence caption; teacher feedback and what changed.

**Additional inputs after a current plan is supplied:** plan title/source/revision; scale if shown; verified dimensions/quantities with location references; annotated extract; production/movement flow; teacher-confirmed checkpoints; one unresolved question.

**Completion:**

- while no current plan and requirement are confirmed, status remains `Teacher to confirm/provide current plan and task requirement` and plan-reading is not marked complete;
- practical evidence requires a caption and teacher observation/checkpoint where applicable; and
- final evaluation names evidence for one strength and one improvement.

**Source:** Cards 1-11 for the course synthesis. The Stage 4 workbook and poultry booklet are supplementary/reference only. A current teacher-issued brief, plan and procedures are required before plan/practical evidence becomes mandatory.

**Boundary:** no inferred scale, dimensions, quantities, layout, animal capacity, safety zone or procedure.

**Outcome tags:** TE4-PPM-01, TE4-DES-01; TE4-SAF-01 only where separately evidenced through teacher-confirmed practical work.

## Student flow

1. Enter name/class once and read the local-save notice.
2. Open the current stage; read/view the exact source-linked theory/stimulus.
3. Complete the answerable stage activity and explanation.
4. Complete the student-learning knowledge check.
5. Add the stage evidence to its matching folio card.
6. Check the saved evidence preview and resolve missing required fields.
7. Move to the next stage; the dashboard shows meaningful completion, not just visited status.
8. Use Card 12 throughout practical work for planning, observed evidence and final evaluation.
9. Review all twelve cards and generate the printable evidence preview.
10. Print / Save PDF and follow the teacher-confirmed submission process.

## Storage and print inputs

- per-card meaningful completion rules;
- JSON data backup and complete image-inclusive export where implemented;
- Print / Save PDF containing all twelve cards, captions, authorised images and missing-evidence notices;
- reset-one and reset-all with confirmation; and
- one-column 390 px controls with keyboard access.

## Review gates

Before formal release, the teacher must confirm the task status/metadata, assessed outcome subset, any extra plan/practical requirement, current plan/drawing, local practical and animal-care procedures, image/privacy rules, approved marking criteria and submission destination. Current verification is also required for published statistics, nutrition claims, industry figures, locations and animal-welfare claims. The absence of these items is a visible authority gate, not permission to guess.
