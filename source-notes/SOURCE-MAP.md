## Executive decision

Use the 11 numbered packages in `/Year 8/2026/` as the authoritative **intended topic order**. Use the current official NSW Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023) as the authority for **outcomes and required learning**. Do not treat the Drive slides or answer copies as factual authorities: they contain numerous unsourced or time-sensitive claims.

The source provides 11 single-lesson packages, not 11 two-week programmes. The minimum honest website mapping is therefore:

> **One Drive package = one website module. “Two-week module” is an integration inference and teacher-adjustable sequence, not a statement found in the source.**

No calendar dates, term allocation or 22-week programme should be invented. Each website module can transparently describe its numbered source lesson as the anchor and use the second week for traceable practice, practical work and evidence only where a valid source or teacher-confirmed brief exists.

Release blockers are: the apparent Module 5 dairy image-answer mismatch; the blank Module 4 design placeholder; absence of a current programme/scope and practical/WHS documentation; absent linked-media URLs; and material syllabus/evidence gaps.

## Current official syllabus map

The 2023 syllabus is implemented from 2026. NESA states that all focus areas are to be delivered across Years 7–8, practical learning and project work occupy most course time, projects collectively cover all content, and students document, communicate and evaluate design and production processes across Years 7–8.

On the NESA Food and agricultural practices page, “Show outcomes” directly links this focus area to:

- `TE4-SDP-01` — explains relationships between sustainability, design and production
- `TE4-PDP-01` — describes practices and processes of designers and producers
- `TE4-DES-01` — communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions
- `TE4-PPM-01` — applies processes in planning, management and production of projects
- `TE4-SAF-01` — selects and safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes

The full Stage 4 outcomes page also lists `TE4-MSC-01`, `TE4-DIG-01` and `TE4-DIG-02`, but the Food and agricultural practices content page does not directly link them. They must not be presented as direct focus-area mappings without a deliberate cross-focus project decision.
