Program control

What this alignment means

Course identity

Year 8 / Stage 4 introductory unit contributing to a 100-hour Agriculture Technology elective. Additional study is required to complete the elective.

Current syllabus

Agriculture Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2024) ↗, implemented from 2027.

Stage 4 adjustment

AGT4-ADJ-01 allows teachers to adjust the Stage 5 outcomes for students in Years 7 and 8. This unit uses AGT5-SAF-01, AGT5-APG-01, AGT5-PRA-01, AGT5-ENV-01, AGT5-IVT-01, AGT5-USE-01 and AGT5-COM-01 where the actual learning and evidence support them.

100-hour course boundary

The complete elective requires at least two enterprises, including one plant and one animal enterprise, one intensive or extensive enterprise, and at least one collaborative activity. This unit does not claim to complete those requirements.

Teacher to confirm before use

  1. Exact period placement of the eleven modules within the confirmed 50-period allocation.
  2. The additional 50 hours and enterprise coverage that complete the 100-hour elective.
  3. Every practical or fieldwork method, facility, animal-care requirement, tool, material, chemical, supervision arrangement and local risk control.
  4. Any current plan, formal assessment notification, rubric, marks, dates or submission route.

The authorised lessons do not establish these local facts, so the website and program must not invent them.

Local timetable placement, practical authority, assessment and faculty approval remain Teacher to confirm.
